# Photos for The Babbling Gourmet website

Every photo on the site is a file in `assets/img/`. To swap in a real photo, save it as a JPG with **exactly the same file name** and replace the placeholder. Nothing in the pages needs to change.

**Two kinds of images**

- **Real photo needed**: anything that shows Andi, her food, the farmhouse or her events. These make a promise about the business, so they must be genuine.
- **Atmosphere image OK (Canva)**: mood shots such as candlelight, linen, ceramics or hands at work. Stock photos are fine here, as long as they don't look like they're presented as Andi's dishes, venue or events.

**Choosing a cohesive set:** warm, low light (candles, window light), natural textures (linen, wood, stoneware), believable food colours, no harsh flash or cool blue tones. Export at the sizes below or larger, as JPG at about 80% quality. Each file should ideally be under 400 KB (a free tool like squoosh.app helps).
