Our food

Tell me what you love to eat

That’s where every menu begins. You might have a favourite cuisine in mind, a dish that brings back memories or a few ingredients you can’t resist.

Chef Andi Marcus brings a love of cooking from scratch and flavours from around the world to every gathering. Menus are custom-made, with as much attention to the people at the table as the occasion itself.

Food you’ll want to gather around

I love the anticipation of a first bite and the moment someone asks for another helping. Whether I’m preparing a plated dinner or filling the table with dishes to share, I want the food to be a memorable part of your time together.

Your menu can draw on familiar favourites, introduce something new or bring a little of both to the table.
