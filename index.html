Have everyone over and leave the cooking to me

Love having people over, but find yourself spending most of the evening in the kitchen? I’d love to help with that.

I’m Chef Andi Marcus, the cook and enthusiastic conversationalist behind The Babbling Gourmet. I’ll work with you to create a menu you’re excited to share, then come to your home or cottage to prepare dinner while you enjoy your guests. Whether it’s a family get-together, a birthday or a weekend with friends, we’ll make sure great food is part of the occasion.

You can also join us at our farmhouse near Renfrew for private dining and celebrations, or let us cater your next team gathering, seminar or corporate event.
