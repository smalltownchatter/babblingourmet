How planning works

A little planning makes for a lovely evening

  1. Tell me what you’re planning

    Share your date, who’s joining you and what kind of gathering you have in mind. You don’t need every detail figured out before getting in touch.

  2. We’ll create the menu together

    We’ll work through the menu around your tastes, including dietary requirements and any favourite dishes you’d like me to consider.

  3. Enjoy the occasion

    You’ll know what’s planned before the day arrives, so you can look forward to welcoming everyone.
