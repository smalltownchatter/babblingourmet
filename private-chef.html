Enjoy hosting while I take care of dinner

Whether you’re gathering friends at the cottage or hosting a celebration at home, I’ll create a menu around your tastes and prepare dinner in your kitchen, leaving you free to enjoy the company. Bring your appetite and settle in for an evening around the table.

A menu that starts with you

Every private chef experience begins with a conversation. Tell me what you love to eat, who’s joining you and what kind of gathering you have in mind.

Perhaps you’d like several courses for a special birthday, a generous dinner to share with family or something delicious to finish a day out with friends. We’ll work through the menu together, including dietary requirements and any favourite dishes you’d like me to consider.

Photo to come · Guests around the table

Make an occasion of being together

You’ve brought everyone together, so you should have time to enjoy them. While I prepare dinner, you can spend the evening with your guests and be part of the conversation from beginning to end.

The familiar comfort of your home or cottage makes the experience personal, whether you’re marking a milestone or simply making the most of a weekend away.

A little planning makes for a lovely evening

Once we’ve discussed your date, location and guest count, we’ll talk through your kitchen setup, the style of meal you’d like and the arrangements for preparing and serving it.

You’ll know what’s planned before the day arrives, so you can look forward to welcoming everyone.
